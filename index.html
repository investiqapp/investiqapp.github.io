How to Read Candlestick Charts

  • Green (Bullish): The stock price went up. The bottom of the body is the open price, the top is the close price.
  • Red (Bearish): The stock price went down. The top of the body is the open price, the bottom is the close price.
  • Wicks (shadows): The thin lines above and below the body show the high and low prices reached during that time period.
